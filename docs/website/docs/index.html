How the app works, briefly

All four calculators read one shared statistics engine, so they agree on what "demand" and "lead time" mean for a given item.

Demand is sales, netted against same-day returns, measured across every calendar day in the history window. In manufacturing companies you can also count production and assembly consumption, so purchased components are planned from real usage rather than from sales they never appear in.

Lead time is resolved from purchase receipt history where it exists — the only source that also yields variability — then from the item's Lead Time Calculation, then from a fallback you set. Safety stock and reorder point always use the same resolution, so the two can never disagree.

Results are written to the standard item planning fields. There is no parallel data model, and standard planning consumes them with no further configuration.

Formulas

Safety stock, covering variability in both demand and lead time:

SS = Z × √( LT × σD² + D² × σLT² )

Reorder point:

ROP = (average daily demand × lead time) + safety stock

Economic order quantity, the Wilson formula:

EOQ = √( 2 × D × S / H )

Where D is annual demand, S the ordering cost and H the annual holding cost per unit. Full derivations and the reasoning behind the defaults are in the FAQ.
