Per-item controls

Two fields on the Item Card Planning tab, for the items that need to differ from your company defaults.

Exclude from Inventory Planning

When ticked, the item is skipped by every calculator, every bulk run, every scheduled job and the dynamic provider. Nothing the app does will ever touch its planning values.

Use it for:

  • Items whose planning values a planner maintains deliberately, from knowledge the ledger does not contain
  • Items under a supplier agreement that fixes order quantities
  • Items in a launch or phase-out where history is actively misleading
  • Anything you have tuned by hand and do not want a nightly job to overwrite
This is the safety catch for scheduled runs.

A nightly job applies to every eligible item. If a value on an item matters to someone, tick this box rather than relying on nobody scheduling a run.

Planning Service Level % (0 = default)

A per-item service level for the safety stock calculation, overriding the company default in setup. Leave it at 0 to use the default. Range 70–99.99 when set.

This is how you do ABC-style differentiation without an ABC module:

ItemTypical levelReasoning
Critical lines — a stockout loses the order or stops production 98–99% The buffer is cheap relative to the consequence
Ordinary stock Company default (95%) Leave at 0
Low-value, easily substituted, or short shelf life 85–90% Carrying cost and obsolescence outweigh the odd stockout
Service level is not linear in cost.

Moving an item from 95% to 99% roughly doubles its safety stock. Raise it where a stockout is genuinely expensive, not across the board — otherwise you have simply bought more inventory.

Actions on the Item Card

The same tab carries the per-item calculator actions — Calculate All Planning Values, the four individual calculators, and a filtered view of the calculation log. See Running the calculators.

Bulk maintenance

Both fields are ordinary item fields, so they can be set for many items at once with the standard Edit in Excel action on the Item List, or through a configuration package.
