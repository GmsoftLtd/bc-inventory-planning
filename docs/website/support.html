Before you contact us

Two things answer most questions faster than we can:

  • Troubleshooting — every result code and warning, and what to do about each
  • FAQ — formulas, methodology, data handling and scope

What to include

For anything about a calculated value, these four things usually contain the whole answer:

  1. The item number
  2. A screenshot of the calculation log entry for that item — it carries every input that produced the result
  3. Your Inventory Planning Setup page
  4. What you expected the value to be, and why

For an error, add the full message text and roughly when it happened.

What we support

The app is free and MIT licensed, and we support it properly: questions about how it works, help interpreting results, bug reports, and guidance on configuring it for your range.

Advice on your wider planning setup, per-location planning, data cleanup or custom development sits outside app support — but we do that work too. Ask, and we will tell you which side of the line your question falls on.

Reporting a security issue

Email support@gmsoft.co.uk with the details rather than opening a public issue, and we will respond directly.
