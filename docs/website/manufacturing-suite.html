Three production-grade extensions covering the inventory, manufacturing and procurement problems that cost the most and get automated the least. Certified on Microsoft AppSource, free 30-day trial, pay-as-you-go.

ABC Classification Manager

Stop treating every item as equally important.

Runs six classification models simultaneously — Revenue, Gross Profit, COGS, Usage Value, GMROII and Pick Frequency — so an item's importance is judged on more than one axis. An item that looks marginal on revenue may be critical on pick frequency, and planning it the same way as everything else is what leaves you overstocked on the wrong things.

  • Six models running at once, not one at a time
  • Interactive worksheets with full classification history
  • Automated cycle counting integration
  • Six reports, six languages

BOM Architect

Production BOM management with the controls the standard BOM lacks.

Standard Business Central lets you change a production BOM. It does not tell you what that change costs, who approved it, or what the previous version looked like. BOM Architect adds cost simulation, approval workflows with audit trails, and full revision control.

  • Cost analysis with what-if scenarios before you commit a change
  • Multi-level approval workflows, with the audit trail to prove it
  • Revision history and component obsolescence warnings
  • CAD and SolidWorks import, and configurable BOM templates

For discrete manufacturing and assembly operations where BOM changes carry cost and compliance consequences.

Vendor Dispute

Recover the money that quietly leaks out of goods receipt.

Shortages, damage, wrong items and quality defects get noticed at the warehouse door and then forgotten, because raising a chargeback is more effort than absorbing the loss. This records the discrepancy in one click from the receipt and generates the chargeback automatically.

  • Four discrepancy types: shortage, damage, wrong item, quality defect
  • Automatic chargeback generation from the warehouse receipt
  • Vendor quality scorecards with A–F grading
  • Configurable approval workflows and SLA tracking

How they fit together

Each stands alone, but they address consecutive links in the same chain: classification tells you which items deserve attention, BOM control governs what you build from them, and vendor discipline governs what arrives in the first place. Run all three and the inventory numbers stop being a matter of opinion.
