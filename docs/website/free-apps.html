Inventory Planning

Safety stock, reorder point, EOQ and reordering policy, calculated from your posted demand history.

Business Central's planning engine does exactly what your item cards tell it to — and on most systems Safety Stock Quantity is empty, Reorder Point was estimated at go-live, and Reorder Quantity is a round number nobody has revisited. This calculates all of them from posted Item Ledger Entries and writes them to the standard fields, so standard planning picks them up with no reconfiguration.

  • Safety stock covering demand and lead-time variability, at a service level you set per item
  • Reorder point, EOQ and the order-up-to level for Maximum Qty. items
  • A recommended reordering policy from a Syntetos–Boylan classification of each item's demand pattern
  • A Planning Worksheet showing current versus proposed values before you apply anything
  • A calculation log recording every input behind every number

Value Entry ↔ G/L Audit

Finds inventory postings that do not match your posting setup.

Audits Value Entries against the General Ledger and flags entries that were posted to accounts other than the ones the inventory posting setup specifies. Ships as a report, a list page and a scheduled email, so the discrepancies come to you rather than waiting to be discovered at year end.

The standalone planning calculators

Superseded by Inventory Planning.

These four came first, each doing one calculation. Inventory Planning replaces all of them with one shared statistics engine, one setup page, one log and a worksheet — so the four no longer disagree with each other about what "demand" or "lead time" means for an item. Use Inventory Planning for new installations. These remain available and MIT licensed.

AppWhat it calculatesSource
Safety Stock Statistical safety stock by the Z-score method, from real demand and lead-time history bc-safety-stock
Reorder Point Demand during lead time plus safety stock, make-to-stock aware bc-reorder-point
EOQ Calculator Economic order quantity by the Wilson formula, from Item Ledger Entries bc-eoq-calculator
Replenishment Policy Advisor Classifies an item's demand pattern and recommends a reordering policy bc-replenishment-policy

Why we give these away

They solve problems we kept meeting on client sites, and they are small enough that charging for them would cost more in administration than it earned. Publishing the source also means you can check exactly what an extension does to your data before you let it near your production environment — which is a reasonable thing to want, and rarer than it should be.

If you need the same rigour applied to something larger, that is what our paid apps and our consultancy work are for.
